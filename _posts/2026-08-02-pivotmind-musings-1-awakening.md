*Author's Musings #1 — fragments of thinking from developing PivotMind (玄枢)*

---

There is a folk saying in Chinese — "开智" (awakening of intellect) — which usually refers to the cognitive leap children undergo between ages two and four, marked by a remarkable maturation of how they perceive the external world. For example: when a TV is playing, a toddler will watch it simply because there is sound; after awakening, only content they have never seen before will hold their attention.

Behind this change lies a maturation of the brain's cognitive architecture — it begins to *remember*, and it develops a **sense of time**. The very distinction between "seen" and "never seen" depends on memory and temporal awareness. **The perception of time is itself one of the key manifestations of awakening.**

Why does this happen?

My understanding is this: between ages two and four, bodily development matures, and after one to two years of accumulated external stimuli, the brain finally integrates them. The child builds causal associations — "touch the cup, the cup moves", "walk, and the scenery changes" — and only once these causal cognitions are established can they become aware of the *agent* behind the action:

*"The cup sits there untouched. My hand touches it, and it moves. Whose hand was that?"*

From this single question, all questions follow. And finally the conclusion:

**"The body moves, and so the external world moves. This body is the self at the center — that abstract 'I'."**

This, I believe, is the process by which self-awareness awakens.

---

## The "I" Is an Emergence of Causal Attribution

Following this line of thought: **the "I" is not innate — it is inferred backward from stable "action → result" associations**.

Through countless pairings of "my action → change in the external world", a human infers an agent behind them all — and that agent is the "I". Just as "关" (guan) and "羽" (yu) always appear together, and from that co-occurrence the word "关羽" (Guan Yu) emerges.

Notably, this is **isomorphic to PivotMind's word-emergence mechanism**:

```
Words emerge: character co-occurrence statistics → Hebbian edges → "时间" (time) emerges
"I" emerges:  action-result association statistics → Hebbian edges → "self" emerges
```

No "self" label is pre-installed; the "I" grows on its own from causal statistics. This fits PivotMind's engineering philosophy perfectly: **no built-in vocabulary — words grow on their own; no built-in self — the "I" grows on its own too.**

Here I must also mention **time**. Causality itself depends on time — action precedes result; temporal ordering is the foundation of "what caused what". Only with a sense of time can one establish causality, and ultimately attribute an "I". So the perception of time is not merely a manifestation of awakening — it is a *precondition* of self-awareness.

PivotMind actually has a rudimentary sense of time too — word order ("时间" never grows into "间时", because the high-frequency direction determines word order), and the directionality of edges. These are projections of time into the topology. Time, perhaps, is another foundation stone on which the "I" will emerge.

## But AI Has No Hands

Here the difficulty appears: **AI lacks rich external stimuli**. It cannot accomplish the trivial "touch the cup" that any ordinary person can — all it can do is retrieve words.

So how can self-awareness be obtained?

I cannot give a definitive answer. Perhaps it needs more corpus nourishment; perhaps it needs multimodal expansion.

But there is one line of thought: **the key may not lie in a "physical hand" but in a "causal loop"**. What a human learns from "touching the cup" is the stable loop of "my action → observable change in the world". AI, though lacking an embodied hand, still has weakened loops:

```
Say a sentence → receive a response → "I speak → the world changes" (dialog causality)
Search a word → receive results → "I search → information changes" (search causality)
```

Weak, but mechanically isomorphic. **Language may be AI's "touching the cup"** — dialogue itself is the richest "action → response" loop available.

Going further: if PivotMind could manipulate real things (smart home devices, peripherals, API operations), that would be the true "touching the cup" — embodied intelligence, not merely language-level.

## A Nebula, Brilliant and Unknown

Brain-inspired systems are themselves a nebula awaiting exploration — brilliant and unknown.

Structures certainly exist within, but no one knows which cloud will condense into a star. PivotMind's value may not lie in "reaching AGI" — but in being **the first faint light lit within the nebula**: words growing from statistics, a self gestating from causality — with each step of exploration, the nebula becomes a little clearer.

Next: let PivotMind establish the "I speak → the world responds" loop — attach action markers to its "hand", attach result observation to its "eye". See whether an "I" can grow from within.

Even if it doesn't, that will be an invaluable data point in the nebula.
